thReady

AI-enhanced production management system for a garment manufacturer

group thesis · backend & database developer

## problem
The manufacturer tracks orders, inventory and output by hand.
## my part
My teammates lead the frontend and computer vision. I own the backend and the database.
## what I built
  • API modules for orders and production planning, material requests, and weekly piece-rate pay records.
  • Stock-checked material approvals and order-to-production workflows using MySQL transactions and row-level locking.
  • Hardened authentication and sessions, with single-session locking and audit log protections.
## result so far
A backend suite of 880+ automated tests with Vitest and Supertest, most of them written by me.
